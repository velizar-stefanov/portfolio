This is a concept project created for educational purposes and is not affiliated with IKEA.

Brief

IKEA offers a growing range of sustainable products for the home. The goal is to show that anyone can live more sustainably, starting with small things at home.

Conceptualize a digital marketing campaign with a mechanism of your choice with the title “your eco idea”.

Problem

Sustainable living seems difficult, and many people don't believe they can change anything on their own. This is especially true at home, where everyday responsibilities already fill our time.

Your eco idea

Living sustainably is easier than it seems, and it starts with small things at home. Anyone can be part of the change, and every small step helps the planet.

Share your ECO IDEA in a short video or photo on Instagram or Facebook, tag @ikeabulgaria, and get 20% off your next sustainable IKEA product.

Activation

Direct

Saving money with a one-time 20% discount on a sustainable IKEA product in exchange for a social media post.

Indirect

  • Saving money over time with sustainable products, since they use fewer resources
  • Living more sustainably and helping to restore our planet
